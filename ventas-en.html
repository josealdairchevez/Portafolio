Data Architecture & Modeling

1. Cleaning & Normalization (ETL)

Transitioned from a flat, redundant database to a normalized system, ensuring referential integrity and optimizing model weight.

  • Transformation and cleaning via Power Query.
  • Primary (PK) and Foreign Key (FK) assignments.
  • Elimination of redundant calculated columns.

2. Relational Model (Star Schema)

Efficient design structuring central information around the transaction log, with one-to-many (1:*) relationships.

  • Fact Table: `Sales` (Transactional).
  • Lookup Tables: `Sellers`, `Location`, `Products`, `Dates` & `Customers`.
  • Single cross-filter direction optimized for performance.

3. Calculations & DAX Language

Implementation of explicit measures to ensure dynamic evaluation against slicers and dimension filters.

Total Sales = SUM(Sales[Sales])

Total Quantity = SUM(Sales[Quantity])

Profit Margin = DIVIDE(
  SUM(Sales[Profit]), [Total Sales]
)

Avg. Discount = AVERAGE(Sales[Discount])

4. Interactivity & Analytical UX

User-centered design through contextual tooltips and dynamic segmentation, allowing deep data discovery without visual overload.

  • Tooltips featuring secondary metrics (% variance against previous/first step).
  • Clean navigation to avoid cognitive fatigue for decision-makers.
  • Synchronous slicers for cross-scenario exploration (Category and Year).
