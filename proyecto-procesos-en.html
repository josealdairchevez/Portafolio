LOGISTICS / PROCESS MODELING

Optimization and BPMN Modeling of Warehouse Flow

Analysis, reengineering, and standardization of logistics processes to eliminate bottlenecks in material dispatch.

Bizagi Modeler BPMN 2.0 Logistics
S

Situation (The Challenge)

During the evaluation of a standard operational model for the reception, storage, and dispatch of raw materials, I identified critical logical flaws in the design. The inventory control mechanism (reorder point review) sequentially blocked the delivery of products that had already been prepared, generating unnecessary bottlenecks in customer service.

T

Task (Objectives)

The objective was to redesign and optimize the diagram using Bizagi Modeler under the international BPMN 2.0 standard. The goal was to achieve a model that accurately reflected the operational reality of an efficient warehouse, guaranteeing uninterrupted order dispatch.

A

Action (Strategy & Modeling)

To resolve the detected operational inefficiency, I applied the following architectural improvements to the model:

  • Logical Restructuring (Parallel Gateway +): Separated the physical product delivery flow from the administrative inventory review flow. This ensures zero delays in dispatch, allowing purchase notifications to occur concurrently.
  • BPMN Nomenclature Standardization: Corrected activities to strictly comply with notation rules, transforming ambiguous nouns into actionable tasks (e.g., "Register transfer between warehouses").
  • Maintenance Flow Integration: Relocated routine tasks ("Material remains stored" and "Maintenance management") to the end of the non-replenishment branch, reflecting warehouse reality without interfering with dispatch.
