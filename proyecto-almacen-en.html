LOGISTICS / PROCESS MODELING

Warehouse Flow BPMN Modeling & Optimization

Analysis, re-engineering, and standardization of logistics workflows to eliminate bottlenecks during material dispatch.

Bizagi Modeler BPMN 2.0 Logistics
S

Situation (The Challenge)

During the evaluation of a standard operating model for raw material reception, storage, and dispatch, I identified critical logical design flaws. The inventory control mechanism (reorder point review) sequentially blocked the delivery of products that had already been fully prepared, creating unnecessary bottlenecks in customer service.

T

Task (Objectives)

The objective was to redesign and optimize the process diagram using Bizagi Modeler under the international BPMN 2.0 standard. The goal was to build a model that accurately reflects the operational reality of an efficient warehouse, guaranteeing uninterrupted order dispatch.

A

Action (Strategy & Modeling)

To resolve the detected operational inefficiencies, I applied the following architectural enhancements to the model:

  • Logical Restructuring (Parallel Gateway +): Separated the physical product delivery workflow from the administrative inventory review workflow. This ensures zero dispatch delays, allowing procurement notifications to occur concurrently.
  • BPMN Nomenclature Standardization: Refined activities to strictly comply with notation rules, transforming ambiguous nouns into actionable verb-first tasks (e.g., "Register inter-warehouse transfer").
  • Maintenance Workflow Integration: Relocated routine tasks ("Material remains in stock" and "Maintenance management") to the end of the non-replenishment branch, realistically capturing warehouse operations without interfering with order fulfillment.
