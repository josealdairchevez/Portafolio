Optimization and BPMN Modeling of Warehouse Logistics Flow

Analysis, reengineering, and standardization of logistics processes using Bizagi Modeler and BPMN 2.0 methodology.

Situation

As part of my professional development in process improvement, I analyzed a standard operational model for the reception, storage, and dispatch of raw materials. During the evaluation of the initial flowchart, I identified critical logical flaws in the design: inventory control (reorder point review) sequentially blocked the delivery of products that had already been prepared, which in a real logistics environment would generate bottlenecks and unnecessary delays in customer service.

Task

The objective was to redesign and optimize the diagram using Bizagi Modeler software under the international BPMN 2.0 standard. The goal was to achieve a model that accurately reflected the operational reality of an efficient warehouse: guaranteeing that order dispatch flows without interruptions, while maintaining strict and concurrent control over inventory levels and maintenance management.

Action

To resolve the detected operational inefficiency, I applied the following architectural improvements to the model in Bizagi:

  • Logical Restructuring using a Parallel Gateway (+): I separated the physical product delivery flow from the administrative inventory review flow. This ensures zero delays in dispatch, allowing purchase notifications to occur concurrently.
  • BPMN Nomenclature Standardization: I corrected the diagram's activities to strictly comply with notation rules, transforming ambiguous nouns into clear actionable tasks (e.g., changing "Inter-warehouse transactions" to "Register transfer between warehouses").
  • Maintenance Flow Integration: I relocated the "Material remains stored" and "Maintenance management" tasks to the end of the logical branch where stock does not require replenishment, correctly reflecting routine warehouse operations without interfering with dispatch.

Result

The result is a highly optimized, clean, and easy-to-interpret BPMN model. This new design documents an agile logistics process where deliveries are made uninterruptedly and replenishment alerts are managed in parallel, preventing stockouts. This project demonstrates my ability to audit workflows, identify operational inefficiencies, and apply standardized technical solutions for the supply chain.
